Let's Learn About the Alien Movies

The first movie, Alien, was released in 1979 and was directed by Ridley Scott and Written by Dan O'Bannon.
The movie follows a crew aboard a massive space hevicle which is en-route to Earth with a load of extraterrestrial ore when they are sidetracked by a strange signal from a nearby planet.
The signals seem to be help calls and when the space ship arrives, they find a ruined moldering spacecraft and within it, an egg-like living organism. Their travel back soons turn to chaos as the egg-like organism hatches and grows into a powerful predator.
