There are a total of 10 Alien movies including the two crossover movies with Predator and the new 2025 TV Series. This page will focus mainly on the first 3 movies, with a few mentions of other movies and crossovers.

ORIGINAL EXNOMORPH SUIT // 1979

Bolaji Badejo is known for his one time acting role where he played the Xenomorph in Alien (1979). Standing at 6 feet 10 inches, his height is what convinced Ridley Scott to cast him.

XENOMORPH // MORTAL KOMBAT X

The Xenomorph appeared as a playable fighter in Mortal Kombat X through a DLC pack. Its weapons include Tarkatan Blades, Inner Jaw, Acid Blood, and Spiked-Tipped Tail. (Side note! My brother and I used to play Mortal Kombat all the time and the Alien was my go to character!)

FILM ARCHIVE : 001

The first movie, Alien, was released in 1979 and was directed by Ridley Scott and Written by Dan O'Bannon.
The movie follows a crew aboard a massive space hevicle which is en-route to Earth with a load of extraterrestrial ore when they are sidetracked by a strange signal from a nearby planet.
The signals seem to be help calls and when the space ship arrives, they find a ruined moldering spacecraft and within it, an egg-like living organism. Their travel back soons turn to chaos as the egg-like organism hatches and grows into a powerful predator.

FILM ARCHIVE : 002

The second movie, Aliens, released in 1986, was directed and written by James Cameron, David Giler, and Walter Hill.
This movie follows Ripley's shuttle after it is found floating in space for 57 years by a deep space salvage team. Upon arriving at LV-426, the marines find only one survivor, a nine-year-old girl named Newt.
But even the battle-hardened marines with all the latest weaponry are no match for the hundreds of Aliens that have invaded the colony.

FILM ARCHIVE : 003

The third movie, Alien 3, was released in 1992, directed by David Fincher and written by Dan O'Bannon, Ronald Shusett, and Vincent Ward.
Alien 3 follows Ellen Ripley who was the only survior when she crash lands on Fiorina 161, a bleak wasteland inhabited by former inmates of te planet's maximum sexurity prison.
Like the two movies before it, Ripley must face skepticism and the Alien as it hunts down the prisoners and gaurds. Without weapons or modern technology, Ripley leads the men into battle against the terrifying and powerful creature.

ADDITIONAL ARCHIVE RECORDS

Alien doesn't stop with just three movies though, after Alien 3, Alien: Resurrection came out in 1997, Alien vs. Predator was released in 2004, Aliens vs. Predator: Requiem was releaed in 2007, Prometheus in 2012,
Alien: Covenant in 2017, Alien: Romulues was released in 2024, and the all new TV Series Alien: Earth released in 2025 with a suspected second season coming around 2027. So lots is still happening in the Alien world despite the first movie premeriing in 1979!

If the movies weren't enough, Alien also has a few video games and crossovers in other video games! Alien: Isolation, Aliens: Fireteam Elite, Aliens: Dark Descent, Alien: Rouge Incursion Evolved Edition, Aliens: Infestation, Alien Trilogy, and Aliens vs. Predator are a few.
Some of these games are even brand new, such as Alien: Rouge Incurion Evolved Edition that released in 2026 for the Virtual Reality.
The Alien/Xenomorph has also made a few crossover appearances in other video games. Dead by Daylight, Mortal Kombat X, Fortnite, Broforce, and several different Predator games all include the Alien/Xenomorph as playable characters, enemies, or an unlockable skin set.
